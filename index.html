Privacy & terms

What we collect. Name, email, mobile number, trip preferences (destination, budget, interests), booking details and the UPI reference number you enter. We use it only to answer you, plan your trip and verify your payment.

What we never collect. Card numbers, CVV, bank passwords or your UPI PIN. Pay inside your own UPI app.

Where it is stored. Submissions are kept in the site owner’s Netlify account and are visible only to the owner. Ask us to delete your data any time using the contact form.

Payments & cancellations. UPI payments are sent to WanderWise’s mobile number and checked by a person against the UTR you submit. A booking shows “Awaiting payment verification” until then. Refunds for cancelled bookings are handled manually and we will call you.

Student project. Packages and prices are sample content for a college project. Confirm availability with us before travelling.
